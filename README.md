> *"From handing out flyers outside the train station to selling out five consecutive nights at the National Dome."*

A rich, standalone custom **Japanese Idol Career** for **The Sims 3**, inspired by modern and classic idol pop culture (*Oshi no Ko*, *Love Live!*, *THE iDOLM@STER*, *Wake Up, Girls!*, and iconic Akihabara / Oricon traditions). 

Begin your journey as an unranked trainee handing out neon photocopied flyers in the rain, claw your way through sweltering underground live houses, and make the pivotal choice at Level 6: debut as a charismatic **Solo Idol** commanding stadium stages alone, or lead a flagship **Idol Group** where formation synchronicity, unit chemistry, and the annual General Election rule all.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Key Features

* **15 Detailed Career Levels**: 5 shared underground/trainee levels, branching at Level 6 into two distinct 5-level tracks (**Solo Idol** and **Idol Group**).
* **Two Custom Lifetime Wishes (Major Dreams)**:
  * 👑 **Living Legend**: Reach Level 10 of the Solo Idol branch (+30,000 Lifetime Happiness Points).
  * ⭐ **National Treasure**: Reach Level 10 of the Idol Group branch (+30,000 Lifetime Happiness Points).
  * Fully integrated into Create-A-Sim (CAS) and the Dreams & Promises manager with custom *Sims 3*-style 54×54 icons.
* **9 Idol-Themed Work Tones**:
  * Train **Athletic** (*Drill Choreography*) or **Charisma** (*Practice Fan-Service*) directly on shift.
  * Gain passive Celebrity Fame while working (*Practice Fan-Service*).
  * Build coworker relationships to boost group harmony (*Bond in Green Room*).
  * Progress toward concert requirements during dress rehearsals (*Rehearse Live Show*).
* **Interactive Live Concerts & Fan Meet-and-Greets**:
  * Book and perform **Live Concerts** at Theatres, Movie Sets, or Stadiums (with a +35% stadium arena box-office bonus!).
  * Host **Handshake & Cheki Meet-and-Greets** to sell merchandise bundles, boost Charisma, and bond with your unit.
  * Sign **Individual Fan Autographs** and host multi-hour **Outdoor Crowd Autograph Sessions**.
* **Idol Group Unit Chemistry System**:
  * Groups automatically populate with fellow unit members. Keeping high relationship scores (LTR ≥ 65) with your unit awards daily performance boosts and the **Pumped** moodlet.
* **Balanced Late Night Celebrity Fame Integration**:
  * Organic fame progression across all 10 levels without strict gatekeeping or artificial promotion walls.
* **Custom Sims 3-Style UI Icons & Vehicles**:
  * Custom 32-bit glossy HUD badges, map tags, and speech balloons.
  * Custom carpools including the **Group Tour Van**, **Sports Car**, and chauffeured **Executive Limousines** (Pink for Solo, Blue for Group).
* **Chance Cards & Custom Opportunities**:
  * 6 interactive dilemma chance cards with branching risk-reward outcomes.
  * 4 reward events and 8 multi-stage career opportunities.

## 🏆 Custom Lifetime Wishes (Major Dreams)

The mod injects two fully functional Lifetime Wishes into Create-A-Sim, the "Change Lifetime Wish" Lifetime Reward menu, and the in-game Dreams & Promises tree:

### 👑 Living Legend (Solo Idol Track)
* **Goal**: Reach Level 10 of the Idol career in the **Solo Idol** branch.
* **Reward**: **30,000 Lifetime Happiness Points**
* **In-Game Description**:
  > *Countless hopefuls step up to the microphone with dreams of stardom, yet only a once-in-a-generation prodigy can command a packed arena stage entirely alone. Your Sim must survive grueling trainee rehearsals, conquer the charts as a Solo Idol, and captivate millions of fans to become a Living Legend.*
* **Recommended Traits**: Virtuoso, Charismatic, Star Quality, Ambitious, Athletic, Natural Born Performer, Diva, Dramatic *(Incompatible with: Shy, Loner)*.

### ⭐ National Treasure (Idol Group Track)
* **Goal**: Reach Level 10 of the Idol career in the **Idol Group** branch.
* **Reward**: **30,000 Lifetime Happiness Points**
* **In-Game Description**:
  > *An idol group shines brightest when every voice and formation step moves in flawless harmony, led by an unshakable Center at the apex of the pyramid. Your Sim must rise from the back row of the rehearsal studio, unite their fellow unit members, and triumph in the General Election to become a beloved National Treasure.*
* **Recommended Traits**: Charismatic, Friendly, Party Animal, Schmoozer, Star Quality, Social Butterfly, Virtuoso, Athletic *(Incompatible with: Loner, Evil)*.

## 📈 Unit Chemistry & Celebrity Fame Systems

### 👥 Idol Group Unit Chemistry
If you choose the **Idol Group** branch:
* Your group is automatically populated with at least 3 agency idols upon promotion to Level 6.
* At the end of every work shift, your Sim's average Long-Term Relationship (LTR) with all unit members is calculated.
* If your average relationship is **≥ 65**, your Sim receives a **+4.5 Career Performance Bonus** and the **Pumped** moodlet!

### ⭐ Balanced Celebrity Fame Integration
No artificial locks or hard fame requirements blocking your career progress:
* Early trainees (Levels 1–2) earn `0` passive Fame from regular shifts.
* Mid and late levels earn organic Fame from daily shifts, promotion bonuses, concerts, handshake events, and opportunities.
* Reaching 5-Star Celebrity is an active, earned accomplishment achieved through live performances and fan engagements.

## 💾 Installation & Requirements

### Requirements
* **The Sims 3 Base Game** patched to version `1.67` or `1.69`.
* **The Sims 3: Late Night Expansion Pack** (required for Celebrity Fame, venues, and limousine mechanics).

### How to Install
1. Download [`TS3_IdolCareer_Mod.package`](TS3_IdolCareer_Mod.package).
2. Place the file into your Sims 3 Mods folder:
   ```text
   Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 3\Mods\Packages\
   ```
3. *(Recommended)* Delete `scriptCache.package` located in:
   ```text
   Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 3\
   ```
   *(This ensures the game reloads custom scripts, Lifetime Wishes, and strings cleanly).*

### How to Join the Career In-Game
* **At Any Venue**: Click any **Theatre** (e.g., *Wilsonoff Community Theatre*) or **Plumbob Pictures Backlot / Movie Set** rabbit hole and select **"Join Idol Career"**.
* **Computer or Newspaper**: Check the daily job listings under **"Find a Job"** or **"Jobs & Professions"** when the Idol career appears in the rotation.

## 🔧 Compatibility, Building & Technical Details

* **NRaas Compatible**: Fully compatible with NRaas MasterController, NRaas Career, NRaas StoryProgression, and Overwatch.
* **Non-Destructive**: This mod is 100% standalone and does not overwrite any core game XMLs or gameplay scripts.
* **Multi-Language Ready**: Includes 22 localized STBL string tables covering all official game language codes (defaults to English).
* **Safe Uninstallation**: To remove the mod, have your Sim quit the Idol career (or retire), save the game, and remove the `.package` file from your `Mods/Packages` directory.

### 🛠️ Building the Package from Source
If you wish to modify tuning XMLs, strings, or icons and rebuild the `.package` file:
1. Ensure [s3pe](http://www.simlogical.com/s3pe.htm) is installed (e.g. `C:\Program Files\s3pe`).
2. Open PowerShell in the `Source/` folder.
3. Run the public build script:
   ```powershell
   powershell.exe -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\BuildPackage_Public.ps1
   ```
   *You can also supply custom arguments such as `-S3PEDir "D:\Tools\s3pe"` or `-OutputPath "path\to\output.package"`.*
